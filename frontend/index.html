The Problem

Concrete design is painfully slow and error-prone

Civil engineers spend hours navigating complex code tables, running manual calculations, and second-guessing compliance.

01

Hours of Manual Calculation

Engineers manually reference ACI tables, perform iterative w/c calculations, and verify results — a process that takes 2-4 hours per design.

02

Multi-Standard Confusion

Different projects demand different codes. Switching between ACI 211, EN 206, and TS 500 is disorienting and risks non-compliance errors.

03

No Audit Trail

Spreadsheets get lost. Calculations aren't reproducible. Handing off projects to colleagues means starting from scratch.

The Solution

Miksir turns conversations into compliant designs

Just describe your project — Miksir handles the code lookup, parameter selection, and compliance checking automatically.

Design History & Export

Features

Everything a concrete engineer needs

🎯

Smart Exposure Class Detection

Automatically identifies the correct exposure class from project description and maps it to code-mandated requirements.

🧮

Aggregate & Admixture Optimization

Recommends optimal aggregate grading, water reducers, and SCM percentages for your strength and workability targets.

Trial Mix Documentation

Multi-Standard Switching

💬

Design Refinement Dialogue

Ask Miksir to adjust strength, reduce cost, or modify workability. It recalculates instantly while maintaining compliance.

Secure Project Storage

All designs encrypted and stored. Access from any device. Share with colleagues or clients via a secure link.
